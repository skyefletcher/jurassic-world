## Project modifications

The optional blue/grey variant uses a new grey skin palette, blue flank/head/tail markings, and darker claws, baked from editable Blender materials. Original mesh anatomy and source scale detail remain. Thirteen skeletal and facial clips are merged into named glTF animations for a standalone Godot review. An export wrapper restores the approved Blender scale, and skin weights are limited to eight influences per vertex for the game engine.

Delta adds a dark-green albedo with darker mottling, generated from editable Blender materials and the source skin detail. The runtime texture overrides only the body material; it shares the approved geometry, scale normal map, eye/claw material, and thirteen animation clips with Blue.

Echo adds an independent brown/ochre body texture with a pale underside, darker mottling and no facial scars. It uses the same geometry, detailed normal map, eye/claw material and animation clips as Blue and Delta. The original assets remain unchanged.

Charlie adds an independent plain olive-green body texture without stripes, with darker face and limbs and a warm grey underside, baked from editable Blender materials. It shares the approved geometry, detailed normal map, eye/claw material and animation clips.
